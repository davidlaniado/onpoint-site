## Who we are

OnPoint Health Media & Tech is a performance platform for telehealth patient acquisition. We run exclusive, high-converting telehealth offers across metabolic and weight management, hormone optimization, men's wellness and preventive health, dermatology, and diagnostics, and we partner with the best media buyers and affiliates in the industry to scale them.

Our founders have driven billions in direct-to-consumer sales for the brands they have scaled. Before OnPoint, they built one of the largest performance marketing companies in the world, with hundreds of team members across seven global offices. OnPoint is their next chapter, and it runs the same way: a small, senior, founder-led team where every person owns their lane and sees their impact in the numbers every day.

## The role

You will be selling exclusive, high-converting, high-yielding telehealth offers to the best media buyers and affiliates in the industry. As our Affiliate Manager, you will own the partner side of OnPoint: finding great partners, getting them live on our offers, and growing them into consistent, high-volume producers.

You will run the full cycle yourself, from prospecting and pitching to negotiating payouts, onboarding, and day-to-day account management. Early on, you will learn our offers cold, build your target list, and get your first new partners signed, live, and scaling on a weekly recruiting rhythm. Your commission is tied directly to the revenue your partners produce.

## What you will do

- Prospect and pitch new affiliates and media buyers through outbound outreach, industry communities, networks, and conferences.
- Negotiate payouts, caps, and terms that work for both sides.
- Onboard new partners with everything they need to launch: tracking links, creatives, offer details, and compliance guidelines.
- Run regular check-ins with active partners, clear their blockers fast, and push for more volume.
- Monitor partner performance daily in Everflow, watching EPC, conversion rates, pacing, and traffic quality.
- Flag fraud or quality issues before advertisers do, and keep both sides aligned.
- Keep caps, payouts, partner records, and the recruiting pipeline organized and up to date.

## What you bring

- 3+ years in affiliate management at a network, an agency, or a direct advertiser.
- An existing network of media buyers and affiliates, ideally in health, telehealth, or DTC.
- Fluency in EPC, CPA, and ROAS, plus comfort in tracking platforms like Everflow, Cake, or HasOffers.
- A closer's mentality. You are motivated by targets and you follow up until the deal is done.
- Organization and self-direction. You take ownership of the function rather than waiting for instructions.
- An understanding that telehealth offers require partners who play by the rules, and you see that as a selling point, not a limitation.

## Bonus points

- Direct experience in telehealth.
- Hands-on media buying experience on Meta, native, or search, so you understand the media buyer's side of the table.
- A known presence on the conference circuit (Affiliate Summit and similar).

## Compensation and why OnPoint

- $100,000+ base salary plus performance-based commission tied directly to the affiliate revenue you bring in.
- Full ownership of the affiliate function.
- You will work directly with the founders.
