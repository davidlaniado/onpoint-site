## The role

We are always talking to strong Meta buyers. If you have scaled direct response offers with your own hands, we want to hear from you even if the timing is not perfect.

You will own campaigns end to end: creative testing, launch structure, daily kill and scale calls, and reporting against the numbers that matter (cost per sale, not cost per click). You will work directly with our Chief Growth Officer and a creative engine that ships new ads every day.

## What you bring

- 2+ years buying on Meta for DTC, lead-gen, or telehealth offers, with real budgets.
- You read a funnel: quiz starts, checkouts, sales, and what each one should cost.
- Comfort with tracking and attribution tools (RedTrack, Everflow, or similar).
- Opinions about creative. You know why an ad works, not just that it does.
- You understand that health advertising has rules and you treat them as part of the job.

## How to apply

Email **david@onpointhealthmedia.com** with a short note on the biggest offer you have scaled, what the numbers looked like, and what you learned.
