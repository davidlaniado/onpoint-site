## The role

OnPoint is hiring a full-time Direct Response Video Editor to produce high-volume, performance-focused video ads for our telehealth brands on Meta and TikTok-style platforms. This is a direct response role, not a traditional editing role. Every edit is built to get attention, hold attention, and drive measurable results.

You will work directly with our Chief Growth Officer, turning proven concepts, scripts, and creative direction into ads that generate revenue. Our creative engine is AI-first: AI-generated UGC, AI voiceover, and AI video built on top of established direct response frameworks. This role offers a clear growth path toward a Creative Strategist position for editors who learn the performance side of the business.

## About OnPoint

We are a fast-scaling telehealth advertiser in one of the fastest-growing categories in DTC right now. OnPoint is run by founders who have driven over $1 billion in revenue for e-commerce and lead-gen brands through data-driven media buying and conversion-focused creative. You will not be learning direct response from people figuring it out as they go. You will be learning it from operators who already built the playbook at scale.

We test aggressively. We launch constantly. We optimize based on data. Our editors are not production assistants. They are a core part of creating winning ads.

## What you will do

- Create direct response video ads for Meta and TikTok-style platforms.
- Build ads using AI-generated content, existing assets, creator content, and stock footage.
- Use AI tools to produce both A-roll and B-roll content.
- Produce multiple creative variations for testing.
- Organize and maintain the creative asset library.
- Upload, name, and document completed ads.
- Iterate on creative based on performance data.
- Stay current with emerging AI tools and editing workflows.

This is a high-volume creative environment where speed and consistency matter.

## Required

- Full-time availability (40+ hours per week).
- Experience creating direct response content for DTC, e-commerce, or telehealth brands.
- Strong understanding of video pacing and attention retention.
- Experience creating content for Meta and TikTok-style platforms.
- Experience using AI tools in an editing workflow.

## Preferred

- AI video generation (Seedance, Veo, Kling, Higgsfield, or similar).
- AI voiceover tools (ElevenLabs or similar).
- Experience in creative testing environments.
- Working knowledge of consumer psychology.
- Health, wellness, or telehealth advertising experience.

## How we measure success

- Minimum 100 ads produced per week.
- 3 to 5% ad hit rate.
- Consistent delivery speed and high execution quality.

## Tools

Use whatever editing software lets you produce winning ads efficiently. Common tools include CapCut, Premiere Pro, After Effects, Seedance, Veo, Kling, Nano Banana Pro, GPT-Image, and ElevenLabs. You will also work hands-on with Claude Code to generate assets and automate the repetitive parts of the job.

## Compensation

Competitive base, set on experience and demonstrated ability, plus a performance bonus tied directly to the results your ads drive. When an ad you make hits real spend, you share in the upside.
