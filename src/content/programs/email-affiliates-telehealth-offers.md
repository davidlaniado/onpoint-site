## Why email partners use OnPoint

Email audiences convert on trust, and trust is exactly what regulated health offers need. OnPoint offers come with approved creative and claim sets so a publisher can promote a telehealth program without guessing what is allowed, and with tracking that credits every sale even when the reader converts days later.

## What you get

- **Approved email creative** per offer: subject lines, body copy, and the claim set behind them.
- **Approved landing pages** that match the email angle.
- **S2S postbacks** on the charged order.
- **CPA payouts**, with **revenue share** available on select offers for owned-audience partners.

## What we need from you

- Opted-in lists with proper unsubscribe handling.
- Creative inside the claim set, reviewed before send.
- Traffic to approved landing pages only.

## What converts in email

Educational, story-driven sends. A publisher who explains what a program is, who it is for, what it costs, and what happens after you order will outperform a hard sell every time. Sequences that warm the reader over two or three sends before the offer perform well in the considered categories.

Apply as a partner, tell us about your list and audience, and we will come back with the offers that fit.
