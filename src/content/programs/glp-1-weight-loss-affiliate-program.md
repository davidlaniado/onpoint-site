## What the offer is

Compounded GLP-1 weight loss programs sold through telehealth. A patient sees an ad, completes a medical intake, a licensed provider reviews the case, and if approved the patient is charged for a monthly program that includes the medication and ongoing care. The brand runs the funnel, the providers, and the pharmacy relationships. OnPoint runs the tracking, the partner side, and the review.

## Who can run it

Media partners who have cleared identity, KYC, and traffic source verification, and who are approved for the channels the offer allows. GLP-1 is the most scrutinized category in telehealth advertising, so this is not an offer for undisclosed sources or borrowed accounts.

Brands in this category can apply to have their GLP-1 program distributed through the network. You keep ownership of your claims and your funnel.

## What partners get

- A brand-approved claim set before you build a single ad, so you know exactly what you can and cannot say.
- Approved landing pages and, on some offers, a choice of pre-sell and direct-to-quiz entry points.
- Server-to-server postbacks on the charged order, so sales that close two days later on another device still get credited to you.
- Funnel event visibility (quiz start, checkout, sale) so you can optimize before the sale data arrives.
- Payment on approved performance under the terms agreed for the offer.

## What converts

Across the GLP-1 programs on the network, the creative that performs is honest, plain-spoken, and specific about the process: real provider, real pharmacy, clear monthly price, what happens after you order. Price-forward creative with a clear value stack outperforms vague "lose weight fast" angles, and it also stays inside the claim set. Spanish-language creative for Spanish-speaking audiences has been a standout, and it works best when the ad says plainly that the care team speaks Spanish.

## What is off limits

- Claims the brand has not approved, including specific weight numbers and guaranteed outcomes.
- Implying the medication is the branded product when the offer is compounded.
- Sending traffic from a channel or placement the offer has not approved.
- Any partner-built checkout or intake that is not the brand's.
