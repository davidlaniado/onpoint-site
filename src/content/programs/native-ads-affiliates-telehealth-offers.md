## Why native buyers use OnPoint

Native is a story channel, and telehealth is a story category. OnPoint offers that support native come with approved pre-sell and advertorial pages, so a native buyer can run a proper headline-to-story-to-offer flow without building the middle page from scratch or guessing which claims will survive.

## What you get

- **Approved pre-sell and advertorial pages** on offers that support the format.
- **A claim set for headlines and thumbnails**, the most policed part of native.
- **S2S postbacks** on the charged order, through the pre-sell page.
- **CPA payouts** on approved orders under agreed terms.

## What we need from you

- Your own native platform accounts, disclosed, meeting the platform's health policies.
- Headlines, thumbnails, and pages inside the claim set.
- No cloaking, no redirects to unapproved pages.

## What converts in native

First-person stories with a specific, relatable detail in the headline and a plain reveal of the process and price on the page. The advertorial format that works is honest about being an ad and still tells a story. Sensational headlines get clicks, fail review, and do not convert to charged orders.
