## Why search partners use OnPoint

Search intent in telehealth is explicit and expensive. A partner bidding on the right terms needs an offer that converts at a rate that justifies the click cost, tracking that credits every sale, and a brand that will not pull the offer the week it starts working. OnPoint offers are verified on both sides and terms are locked before launch.

## What you get

- **Offers with explicit intent**: diagnostics, TRT, hair loss, GLP-1, and ED.
- **Direct-link tracking** where the offer allows it, plus approved landing pages.
- **A keyword and brand-term policy per offer**, so there are no surprises.
- **S2S postbacks** on the charged order and funnel events in reporting.
- **CPA payouts** on approved orders under agreed terms.

## What we need from you

- Your own Google Ads accounts, disclosed, meeting whatever certification the brand requires.
- No bidding on restricted brand terms.
- Ad copy inside the claim set.

## What converts on search

Landing pages that match the query. A person searching for a specific treatment wants the price, the process, and proof of a real provider on the first screen. The approved pages on the network are built for that. Partners who segment by intent (condition terms vs treatment terms vs brand-adjacent terms) and route each to the matching page consistently outperform.
