## What you are buying

Distribution to verified media partners, plus everything that makes distribution safe in a regulated category: partner verification, creative review against your claim set, server-side tracking, and payout reconciliation. You keep your funnel, your providers, and your claims.

## How it works

1. **Verification.** Business, credential, and compliance review.
2. **Offer intake.** Your claim set, allowed channels, landing pages, and commercial terms (CPA, revenue share, or hybrid), agreed before launch.
3. **Funnel and creative review.** We check the entry points and the approved creative structures against advertising standards.
4. **Tracking setup.** Click ID passed through your funnel, server-to-server postback on the charge, reversal events for refunds and chargebacks.
5. **Launch** with verified partners on approved channels only.
6. **Reporting and optimization.** Partner-level attribution, funnel events, and support as you scale.

## What you control

- Every health claim. We review for standards; we never write or change a medical claim.
- Which channels and placements are allowed.
- Which partners run your offer.
- Caps, payouts, and approval rules.

## What we handle

- Partner verification and monitoring.
- Creative review before launch and removal of sources that fall below standard.
- Tracking, reporting, and payout reconciliation through Everflow and connected systems.
- Partner support, so your team is not fielding tracking questions at 11pm.

## Categories we support

Metabolic and weight management, hormone optimization, men's wellness, dermatology, hair and skin, and at-home diagnostics.

Apply as a brand. Tell us the category, the funnel, and where you are stuck, and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.
