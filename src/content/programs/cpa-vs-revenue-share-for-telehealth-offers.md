## The three structures

**CPA.** A fixed payout per approved, charged first order. The partner carries the traffic risk; the brand carries the retention risk. Simple to model, and the default for new offers.

**Revenue share.** A percentage of what the patient actually pays over time, including renewals. The partner earns more from patients who stay. Only works with tracking that covers rebills and refunds, which OnPoint runs through Everflow.

**Hybrid.** A smaller fixed payout on the first order plus a share of later payments. The usual landing spot for offers that started on CPA and matured.

## How OnPoint decides which to offer

The brand chooses what it will pay per acquisition and whether it will share renewal revenue. OnPoint sets partner payouts below that and matches structure to partner type: media buyers running paid traffic usually need CPA for cash flow; owned-audience partners often prefer revenue share.

## What has to be true for any of them

- Terms in writing before launch: payout, qualifying event, cap, disqualifiers, payment timing.
- One source of truth for conversions, visible to both sides.
- A clear definition of "approved" and how reversals work.

## A note on reporting

When OnPoint reports program economics to a brand, the number that matters is what the brand pays per approved acquisition, not the affiliate payout. Those are different numbers, and mixing them up is the most common source of confusion in this business.
