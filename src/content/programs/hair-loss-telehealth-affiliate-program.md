## What the offer is

Telehealth programs for hair loss. The patient completes an intake, a licensed provider reviews it, and approved patients receive prescription and over-the-counter treatments on a subscription, often finasteride, minoxidil, or a combination. Some brands also offer supplements and topical products alongside.

## Who can run it

Verified media partners approved for the offer's channels. Hair loss is a considered purchase with a long research phase, so partners with owned audiences (email, content) do especially well alongside paid social.

Brands with a hair loss program can apply to distribute through the network.

## What partners get

- The brand's approved claim set, including approved imagery rules.
- Approved landing pages and quiz entry points.
- Server-to-server postbacks and funnel event visibility.
- Payment on approved performance under agreed terms.

## What converts

Honest, educational creative. The audience has usually read a lot already and can smell hype. Ads that explain what the medications actually do, what to expect and when, and what the monthly cost is outperform anything promising fast regrowth. Static formats with a clear price and a "here is how it works" structure have been reliable.

## What is off limits

- Regrowth guarantees, timelines, or percentage claims the brand has not approved.
- Unapproved before-and-after imagery.
- Fear-based creative about hair loss that the brand has not approved.
- Undisclosed traffic sources.

## How to start

Apply as a partner with your channels. Brands: apply as a brand and we will start with verification and claims review.
