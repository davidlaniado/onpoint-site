## What the offer is

Telehealth programs for women in perimenopause and menopause. The patient completes an intake, a licensed provider reviews it, and approved patients receive hormone replacement therapy and ongoing care on a monthly program. Some brands use an introductory first-month price to make the first order easier.

## Who can run it

Verified media partners approved for the offer's channels. This audience is underserved and responsive, and it is also one that has been dismissed by the medical system for years, so the tone of the creative matters as much as the claim set.

Brands with a women's hormone health program can apply to distribute through the network.

## What partners get

- The brand's approved claim set, including how symptoms can be described.
- Approved landing pages and intake entry points.
- Server-to-server postbacks and funnel event visibility.
- Payment on approved performance under agreed terms.

## What converts

Validation first. Creative that says "you are not imagining this" and then explains what a provider can actually do performs far better than clinical or salesy angles. Plain-language explanations of what the program includes, what it costs, and that the first month is easy to try have been the reliable structure.

## What is off limits

- Cure, prevention, or reversal claims the brand has not approved.
- Shortage or scarcity claims that have not been verified and approved.
- Fear-based creative.
- Undisclosed traffic sources.

## How to start

Apply as a partner with your channels. Brands: apply as a brand.
