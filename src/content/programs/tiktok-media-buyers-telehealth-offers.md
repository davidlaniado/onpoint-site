## Why TikTok buyers use OnPoint

TikTok is the hardest major platform for telehealth creative, and the one with the most upside for buyers who can get through review. OnPoint's founders run TikTok for telehealth offers themselves, so the offers that allow the channel come with creative structures and bridge pages built for TikTok's rules.

## What you get

- **A clear list of offers that allow TikTok** and what each requires.
- **Creative structures built for TikTok review** and the claim set behind them.
- **Bridge pages** where the brand uses them.
- **S2S postbacks** on the charged order and funnel events in reporting.
- **CPA payouts** on approved orders under agreed terms.

## What we need from you

- Your own TikTok ad accounts, disclosed, meeting the platform's health requirements.
- Creative inside the claim set.
- Traffic to approved pages only.

## What converts on TikTok

Video that looks and sounds like it belongs on the platform: one person, one clear point, no polish. Fast first lines that name the problem plainly. Honest process explanations. The same rules as Meta, applied harder.
