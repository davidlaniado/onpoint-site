## Why Meta buyers use OnPoint

Meta is where most telehealth acquisition happens, and it is also where most health creative gets rejected. OnPoint's founders buy Meta media for telehealth offers every day, so the offers on the network come with the two things a buyer actually needs: a claim set that has survived review, and landing pages that convert cold traffic.

## What you get

- **Offers across six verticals**: GLP-1 weight loss, ED, TRT, hair loss, women's hormone health, and at-home diagnostics.
- **Platform-safe claim sets** per offer: what has been approved, what gets rejected, and why.
- **Approved landing pages**, including pre-sell pages on offers that support them.
- **Server-to-server postbacks** on the charged order, with funnel events (quiz start, checkout) reported so you can optimize before sales arrive.
- **Sub ID support** so you can see performance by campaign, ad set, and ad in your reporting.
- **A partner team that runs Meta itself** and can tell you what is getting through review this week.

## What we need from you

- Your own ad accounts, disclosed. No borrowed or rented accounts.
- Creative that stays inside the claim set. If you want to say something new, ask and we will take it to the brand.
- Traffic to the approved landing pages only.

## What converts on Meta right now

Across the network, the consistent winners are plain-spoken video with a strong first three seconds and a clear price, first-person long-form static "advertorial" images, and formats that feel native to the feed (a reply to a comment, a product on a shelf, a text-message screenshot style). Hype does not survive review and does not convert. Honest does both.

## How payouts work

CPA on approved, charged first orders, at the rate on the offer sheet. Approval rules and reversal reasons are visible in reporting. Payment timing follows the agreed terms.
