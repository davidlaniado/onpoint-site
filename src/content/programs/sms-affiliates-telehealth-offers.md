## Why SMS partners use OnPoint

SMS is a high-response channel with real legal exposure, which is why many health brands refuse it outright. OnPoint works with SMS partners on the offers that allow it, with clear rules on consent, copy, and landing pages, so the channel can run at scale without putting the brand or the partner at risk.

## What you get

- **A clear list of which offers allow SMS** and what each requires.
- **Approved message copy and claim sets** per offer.
- **Approved landing pages** built for mobile.
- **S2S postbacks** on the charged order, so conversions that happen later still credit to you.
- **CPA payouts** on approved orders under agreed terms.

## What we need from you

- Documented, compliant opt-in consent for every number you message.
- Immediate opt-out handling.
- Messages inside the claim set, reviewed before send.
- Traffic to approved landing pages only.

## What converts on SMS

Short, direct, and honest. A plain offer with the price and a clear next step outperforms cute copy. Messages that read like a person, not a broadcast, and that respect the reader's time convert and keep complaint rates low.

## How to start

Apply as a partner, tell us the size and source of your lists and your consent process, and we will come back with the offers that allow SMS.
