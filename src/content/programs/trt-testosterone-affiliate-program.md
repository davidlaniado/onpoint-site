## What the offer is

Telehealth programs for men with symptoms of low testosterone. The patient completes an intake, often does lab work, and a licensed provider decides whether treatment is appropriate. Approved patients are charged for a monthly program that includes medication and ongoing care. Depending on the brand, the medication is an FDA-approved oral testosterone product or a compounded preparation.

## Who can run it

Verified media partners approved for the channels the offer allows. TRT sits in the men's health category that ad platforms watch closely, so disclosed sources and approved claims are non-negotiable.

Brands running a TRT or hormone optimization program can apply to distribute it through the network.

## What partners get

- The brand's approved claim set, including which symptoms can be named and how.
- Approved landing pages built for the intake flow.
- Server-side postbacks on the charged order, with funnel events (intake start, checkout) visible in reporting.
- Clear rules on what happens when a patient needs labs before treatment, so you know when the payable event fires.
- Payment on approved performance under agreed terms.

## What converts

Men in this audience have usually been told their symptoms are "just age." Creative that validates that experience, explains what low testosterone actually is, and describes the process plainly performs. Voice-of-customer language matters: the words men use for the problem, not clinical jargon. One note from experience: some products in this category carry specific side-effect profiles, and the claim set will tell you what you cannot say about them. Follow it.

## What is off limits

- Claims about muscle gain, fat loss, or performance the brand has not approved.
- Implying anyone will be prescribed testosterone regardless of labs or provider judgment.
- Comparing to or naming competitor products in ads.
- Undisclosed traffic sources.

Apply as a partner with your channels and history. Brands: apply as a brand and we will start with verification and claims review.
