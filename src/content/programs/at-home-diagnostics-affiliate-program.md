## What the offer is

At-home lab testing and diagnostics: the patient orders a kit, provides a sample at home or at a partner lab, and receives results reviewed by a licensed provider. Common panels include hormone, metabolic, and general wellness markers. Many brands use diagnostics as the front door to a treatment program (for example, testosterone or metabolic health), which changes the economics for partners.

## Who can run it

Verified media partners approved for the offer's channels. Search and email partners do especially well here because the intent is explicit.

Brands with a diagnostics or lab testing program can apply to distribute through the network.

## What partners get

- The brand's approved claim set for what the test measures and what it does not.
- Approved landing pages and order flows.
- Server-to-server postbacks on the charged order, and where applicable on the follow-on treatment event.
- Payment on approved performance under agreed terms.

## What converts

Clarity about what you get: which markers, how the sample works, how fast results come back, and that a real provider reviews them. Curiosity-driven angles ("find out what is actually going on") work when they are honest about the limits of the test.

## What is off limits

- Diagnosis claims.
- Claims about accuracy or lab certification the brand has not approved.
- Implying results guarantee treatment.
- Undisclosed traffic sources.

Apply as a partner with your channels. Brands: apply as a brand.
